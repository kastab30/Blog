About Me

"Security is not a product, but a process."

Mr. Kastab Garai is a renowned ethical cyber expert whose journey began in 2020, driven by curiosity, discipline, and a strong commitment to digital security. Beyond technology, his story is one of perseverance, continuous learning, and resilience. Each challenge has shaped his character and strengthened his vision. Through this blog, he shares not only cybersecurity insights but also the experiences and lessons that have defined his journey, inspiring others to grow with integrity and determination.
